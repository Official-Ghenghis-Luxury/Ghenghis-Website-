The People Behind the Brand

Leadership Team

Home/Leadership Team

Driven by Vision,
Defined by Excellence

At Ghenghis Luxury, our leadership brings together decades of expertise in design, construction, and international business. Every space we create is a reflection of the passion and precision that our team brings to every project — from Lagos to London.

Amarachi Godspower 🇳🇬

Chief Executive Officer

Amarachi Godspower is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of Ghenghis Luxury, bringing over a decade of experience in luxury interior design and premium construction across Nigeria. A visionary entrepreneur with a passion for elevated living, she built Ghenghis Luxury from the ground up — establishing it as one of Nigeria's most distinguished names in bespoke interiors, doors, POP, tiling, plumbing, architecture, and screading.

Her design philosophy is rooted in the belief that every space should tell a story — one of craftsmanship, authenticity, and enduring quality. Under her leadership, Ghenghis Luxury has shaped some of Lagos's most iconic residential and commercial interiors, earning a reputation built entirely on excellence and client trust.

Esther Abiodun 🇬🇧

Director of International Operations

Esther Abiodun serves as Director of International Operations at Ghenghis Luxury, overseeing the company's global partnerships, procurement pipelines, and cross-border project delivery. Based between Lagos and the United Kingdom, she bridges the gap between Nigerian craftsmanship and international luxury standards.

With a background in international business and a keen eye for premium finishes sourced globally, Esther ensures that every Ghenghis Luxury project meets world-class specifications. Her role is instrumental in the brand's growing footprint across international markets, connecting Nigerian talent with global opportunity and bringing the finest materials and ideas home.

"We don't just build spaces — we build legacies. Every project is a promise kept to our clients and a testament to what luxury truly means."
— Amarachi Godspower, CEO, Ghenghis Luxury
